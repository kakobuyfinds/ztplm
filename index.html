03 / A useful browsing routine

Make your shortlist earn its place.

A longer list is not automatically a better list. For Kakobuy spreadsheet finds, keep a few clear comparison notes before deciding what deserves a closer look.

  1. Choose one category

    Start with shoes, a layer or an accessory. Keep your intended use and preferred fit in view.

  2. Read the exact listing

    Check the selected variation, measurements, stated materials and what is included. Do not rely on a spreadsheet title alone.

  3. Compare useful evidence

    Look for clear photos and consistent descriptions. If a measurement or material is missing, ask the seller before ordering.

  4. Review the complete cost

    Where applicable, account for delivery, service fees, taxes and return conditions. Recheck current information at the destination.

06 / Clear answers

Before you browse.

Short answers to common questions about spreadsheet shopping, product comparisons and this guide.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a shopping reference that organizes product links and comparison notes. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy, freshness and detail of the individual entries.

Is this the official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide linking to CAFANS categories. It does not represent Kakobuy or the brands mentioned on this page.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

For shoes, compare the size chart, stated materials and clear product photos. For clothing, compare garment measurements, fabric details and fit information. A familiar size label alone is not enough.

Do brand names or illustrations prove authenticity?

No. Brand names and editorial illustrations do not authenticate a product. Verify the seller, provenance, listing details and applicable return terms independently.

Where do the category links go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing buttons open https://www.cafans.com.
